About Us

Why DR Liquidity?

A hub for prop firm traders. Built by traders, for traders.

DR Liquidity is a hub for prop firm traders. We built it because the trading space is full of hype, scams, and shallow content. We wanted something different.

What we are

A transparent platform with the tools serious traders need — a real trade journal, prop firm comparisons with verified discount codes, education that doesn't waste your time, and a community that doesn't spam signals.

What we are NOT

A signal service, a "guru" selling dreams, or a platform that hides fees. We make money when you save money on prop firm challenges (affiliate commissions). That's it. No funny business.

The Journal

Is yours, forever. Track every trade, see your equity curve, find what setups work, kill what doesn't.

The Community

Is for sharing real trades, real lessons, and getting feedback. Verified buyers get a badge so you know who's actually traded with the firm they're reviewing.

Discipline over dopamine.
Process over payout.
